Capstone time in the plan: a 30-minute dataset clinic (in week 8), about 280 minutes of independent work, a 25-minute peer dry run of the oral check, the 10-minute check, 5 minutes of changeover and 10 of written feedback from the assessor.

The submission

A notebook in a public repository, then ten minutes with the assessor

  • A public image, ratings or embedding matrix, with its address and licence
  • The matrix stated: rows, columns, shape, centring and scaling
  • The SVD, checked on a small case by hand
  • Relative error by rank, the rank chosen, the leading components interpreted

Submission

The link to a public GitHub repository holding the notebook, its outputs and the data source. Nothing personal, nothing whose licence forbids sharing.

The capstone is AI-full: an assistant may help with any step. The oral check is where authenticity is tested: the participant must explain and redo a step without it.

Format

Ten minutes each: four to walk through, six to rework a step

1 · The matrix

What the rows and columns are, and why you centred or scaled.

2 · The result

The error by rank, the rank chosen, what the leading components show.

3 · The rework

The assessor gives new numbers for one step; you redo it by hand and check it in code.

Peer feedback. After each check, everyone writes one strength, one question and one suggestion.

Choose the step to rework from the participant's own notebook: a centring, a 2 × 2 SVD, a rank-k error from a list of singular values, a projection or a variance share. The new numbers are in the trainer pack, not in this repository.

Rehearse the rework

A rework looks like this: new numbers, the same step, by hand and in code

"Your singular values were replaced by 12, 5, 2 and 1. What relative error does rank 2 give?"

By hand: √(2² + 1²) / √(12² + 5² + 2² + 1²) = √5 / √174 ≈ 0.170

s = np.array([12, 5, 2, 1.0])
tail = (s[2:]**2).sum()
np.sqrt(tail / (s**2).sum())
# 0.1695

A rehearsal example only; the items used in the checks are different and private. Run it with the room before the first check.
