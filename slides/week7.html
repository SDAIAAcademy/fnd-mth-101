What does power iteration converge to?

You repeat x ← A x / ‖A x‖ for a symmetric A with eigenvalues 7, 3 and −2, from a random start. What does x approach?

The eigenvector of −2
The eigenvector of 7, up to sign
An average of the three eigenvectors
Nothing: dividing by the norm each step prevents convergence
Why. The part along the eigenvector of 7 grows by 7 each step, the others by at most 3: the ratio 3/7 shrinks them away.

Answer B. Ask how fast: the error shrinks by about 3/7 per step.

Block A · the SVD

Every matrix is a rotation, a stretch along the axes, and another rotation

1 · Vᵀturn the input onto the axes
→
2 · Σstretch each axis by σᵢ ≥ 0
→
3 · Uturn onto the output directions

A = U Σ Vᵀ, for any m × n matrix. U and V have orthonormal columns; the singular values σ₁ ≥ σ₂ ≥ … ≥ 0.

Contrast with week 6: eigenvectors need a square matrix and may not be orthogonal; the SVD needs nothing. For a symmetric matrix with non-negative eigenvalues the two coincide.

By hand

The singular values are the square roots of the eigenvalues of AᵀA

A = [[2, 2], [−1, 1]]

AᵀA = [[5, 3], [3, 5]], eigenvalues 8 and 2

σ₁ = √8 ≈ 2.828, σ₂ = √2 ≈ 1.414

U, s, Vt = np.linalg.svd(A)
s                     # [2.8284, 1.4142]
np.allclose(U @ np.diag(s) @ Vt, A)

From slide_numbers.py, week 7. AᵀA = V Σ² Vᵀ: week 6's eigen-decomposition of a symmetric matrix. Never compute an SVD this way on real data: forming AᵀA squares the condition number, as in week 5.

Checkpoint

Reading singular values

A 500 × 40 feature matrix has singular values 310, 52, 9, … , 0.004, 0.000002. What does that tell you?

All 40 directions are equally informative
The condition number is 310
Full rank but nearly rank-deficient: cond ≈ 1.6 × 10⁸, unsafe for the normal equations
The rank is 3
Why. 310 / 0.000002 = 1.55 × 10⁸; squared, it reaches the limit of float64.

Answer C. A numerical rank with a tolerance would drop the last value; that choice is the analyst's, and lstsq makes it with its rcond.

Block B · low rank

A is a sum of rank-one layers, largest first: keep the first k

A = σ₁ u₁v₁ᵀ + σ₂ u₂v₂ᵀ + …

A_k keeps the first k layers.

Each layer is an outer product: one column pattern times one row pattern.

Eckart-Young

No rank-k matrix is closer to A than A_k, and ‖A − A_k‖²_F = σ²ₖ₊₁ + … + σ²ₙ. The error is known before you build A_k.

The theorem is stated, not proved. The lab checks it numerically on an image.

The trade

A rank-k copy stores k(m + n + 1) numbers instead of m × n

4.5%
storage of a rank-20 copy of a 1,000 × 800 matrix: 36,020 numbers of 800,000
  • Small k saves space only when the σ fall fast
  • Past some k, the copy is larger than the matrix
  • Choose k from the error curve, not by habit

Block C · PCA through the SVD

Centre the data, take its SVD: the rows of Vᵀ are the principal components

Xc = X - X.mean(axis=0)
U, s, Vt = np.linalg.svd(Xc, full_matrices=False)
components = Vt                 # one per row
share = s**2 / np.sum(s**2)     # variance share
scores = Xc @ Vt[:2].T          # 2-D view
  • Same as week 6's eigenvectors of the covariance
  • Without forming the covariance, so better rounding
  • σ² / (n − 1) are the variances along each component

Show the identity Xcᵀ Xc = V Σ² Vᵀ in one line. Libraries such as scikit-learn's PCA do exactly this.

Interpreting

A component is a pattern of weights over the original features

  • For images: each component is itself an image; large weights mark pixels that vary together
  • The sign is arbitrary: flip a component and its scores flip with it
  • Look at which items score highest and lowest on it, then name what separates them
  • A component explains variance, not meaning: interpretation is a claim to check

The capstone rubric gives a quarter of the marks to interpretation "in the dataset's own terms, with their limits". Say what that means: name the pattern, show the evidence, and say what it does not show.

Checkpoint

A component that points at the mean

A colleague takes the SVD of a ratings matrix without centring it. The first singular value is huge and the first right singular vector has nearly equal entries. What happened?

The first layer captures the average rating, not a pattern of variation
Every item is rated the same by every person
The matrix has rank 1
The SVD routine has failed
Why. Without centring, the mean is the largest common direction; PCA needs the spread around the mean.

Answer A. The lab's image is not centred either: its first layer is the average brightness. That is fine for compression and wrong for PCA.

Week 7 · wrap-up

Three things to keep

Every matrix has an SVD

Rotate, stretch, rotate. Σ gives rank, norm and condition number.

Truncate with a known error

The error of A_k is the tail of the singular values, and no rank-k matrix does better.

PCA is a centred SVD

Centre, decompose, read the components as patterns; interpret with care.
